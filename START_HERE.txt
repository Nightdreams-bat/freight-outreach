FREIGHT OUTREACH
================

FIRST TIME
----------
1. Double-click  Setup.exe
   Answer the questions (your name, company, one-line pitch, path to your
   leads spreadsheet, sending limits). This creates config.json in this folder.

2. Make sure  client_secret.json  is in this folder.
   (If you see a file called "PUT client_secret.json HERE.txt", you still need
    to add it - it's the Google sign-in file.)

3. Double-click  FreightOutreach.exe
   Your browser opens the dashboard. Go to Settings -> "Connect Gmail" and sign
   in with the Google account that will send the emails.

EVERY DAY AFTER THAT
--------------------
Just double-click  FreightOutreach.exe  - it opens the dashboard where you
manage leads, review what's queued, and send. Close the small black window to
stop it.

WHAT THE FILES ARE
------------------
  Setup.exe            first-time setup wizard (run once)
  FreightOutreach.exe  the app - opens the dashboard
  config.json          your settings (created by Setup.exe; safe to back up)
  client_secret.json   Google sign-in file (needed for "Connect Gmail")
  clients.xlsx         your leads (created on first run if it doesn't exist)
  _internal\           the app's engine - don't touch or delete
  Source code\         the full source, if you or a developer want to read it

Everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the emails you
choose to send through your own Gmail.
