FREIGHT OUTREACH
================

START HERE
----------
1. Double-click  FreightOutreach.exe
   Your browser opens the dashboard. A small black window stays open in the
   background - closing it stops the app.

2. Go to the Settings page (left menu) and fill in:
   - Business details: your name, company, phone, one-line pitch.
   - Excel file: click Browse and point it at your leads spreadsheet.
     (If you don't have one yet, leave it - a blank clients.xlsx is created
      next to FreightOutreach.exe on first use.)
   - Lead spreadsheet columns: the app guesses which column is the name,
     company, email and phone. Check the guesses; fix any it got wrong.
   - Gmail account: click "Connect Gmail" and sign in with the Google account
     that will send the emails. Nothing is typed here - it's the real Google
     screen. You need  client_secret.json  in this folder for this to work
     (if you see "PUT client_secret.json HERE.txt", you still need to add it -
      ask whoever set this up).

That's it. There is no separate setup program - everything is on Settings and
can be changed any time.

EVERY DAY AFTER THAT
--------------------
Double-click  FreightOutreach.exe  - it opens the dashboard where you manage
leads, review what's queued, and send.

THE REPLIES PAGE
----------------
When a lead replies, the app reads it and prepares a calendar invite + email
(or a "here are some times that work" email). Nothing is sent or booked until
you click Approve on the Replies page.

To turn this on, in Settings:
  - Click "Connect Gmail" again (it needs extra permissions to read mail and
    your calendar).
  - Paste an Anthropic API key in the "Reply handling" section, then Enable.
    Ask whoever set this up for the key.

WHAT THE FILES ARE
------------------
  FreightOutreach.exe  the app - opens the dashboard
  client_secret.json   Google sign-in file (needed for "Connect Gmail")
  config.json          your settings (created automatically; safe to back up)
  clients.xlsx         your leads (created on first use if you don't pick one)
  reply_queue.jsonl    drafted follow-ups waiting for your approval (auto-managed)
  _internal\           the app's engine - don't touch or delete
  Source code\         the full source, if you or a developer want to read it

Everything runs on your PC. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the emails you
choose to send through your own Gmail, and (if you turn on reply handling) the
text of a reply sent to Anthropic for a yes/no reading.
