Business details
Sender name is who the email is from - shows first in the recipient's inbox.
Company is appended to the From line right after your name (e.g. "Alex Carter - Carter Freight Solutions"), so recipients see the business, not just a bare Gmail address.
Phone is entirely optional - leave it blank and it's skipped cleanly everywhere, no empty line left behind in the signature.
Pitch is a short description of what you do - available as a variable in the templates below, not required to use it there.

Sending limits
Reminder check interval - how often the automation wakes up and looks for meetings ~24h away.
Reminder window - how wide that "~24h" net is. Kept equal to the interval so two consecutive checks always overlap and nobody falls through the gap between them.
Max reminders per run - a safety brake. If one scan ever matches more than this, it sends none and warns you instead, in case of a data mistake (e.g. many rows sharing one date).
Daily send cap - total cold + reminder emails allowed per day, combined. Keeps the account well under Gmail's own daily limit so it doesn't get flagged as spam.
Meeting length - how long a booked call is, and the size of the slots offered when proposing times.
Minimum notice - never propose or accept a slot sooner than this many hours from now.
Scheduling window - how far ahead to look for open slots when proposing times.
