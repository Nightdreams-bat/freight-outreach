Kairo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Kairo is a desktop application that a single operator installs and runs on their own Windows computer to manage their own outbound email. This policy explains what data Kairo touches and where it goes.

Who runs Kairo

Kairo is not a hosted service. There is no Kairo server and no Kairo account. Each copy is operated solely by the person who installed it, on their own machine, using their own Google account.

Data Kairo accesses

When the operator connects their Google account, Kairo requests these permissions and uses them only for the purposes stated:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send) — to send the outreach, follow-up, reminder and scheduling emails that the operator has configured.
  • Read your email (gmail.readonly) — to detect replies from the operator's own leads so a draft response can be prepared. Kairo reads only messages in threads with those lead addresses.
  • Manage calendar events (calendar.events) and view free/busy (calendar.freebusy) — to propose meeting times that don't clash and, on the operator's approval, create the calendar invite.
  • Basic profile email (userinfo.email, openid) — to show which account is connected.

Where data goes

  • All lead data, settings, logs and the Google sign-in token stay in a single folder on the operator's computer (%APPDATA%\Kairo). The token is held in the Windows Credential Manager.
  • Nothing is transmitted to the developer of Kairo. There is no telemetry, no analytics, and no email open- or click-tracking.
  • Email content is sent to Google (to deliver the message) and, only if the operator enables the optional reply-classification feature, the text of an incoming reply is sent to Anthropic's API to categorise it. No other third party receives any data.

Google Limited Use disclosure

Kairo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Gmail and Google Calendar is used only to provide the user-facing features described above, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, for security, or to comply with applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Because all data is local, the operator controls retention entirely. Disconnecting the Google account in Kairo's settings, or deleting the %APPDATA%\Kairo folder, removes the stored token and data. The operator can also revoke Kairo's access at any time from their Google account permissions page.
