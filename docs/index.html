Kairo

A local, single-operator cold-email, follow-up and reply-handling tool for Windows.

Kairo runs entirely on one person's own computer. It reads leads from an Excel file on that PC, sends personalised outreach emails through the operator's own Gmail account, chases non-responders, and — optionally — reads the replies and drafts a scheduling response for the operator to approve. Nothing is sent or booked without a human clicking approve.

  • No server, no database, no hosting — everything lives in one folder on the operator's machine.
  • Email is sent through the operator's own Google account over a standard OAuth sign-in.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no open/click tracking.
